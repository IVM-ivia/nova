Phase 01

Concept & Brand Strategy

Building the semantic foundation of the company — before any visual work began.

  • Developed the name Invest Nova LG and brand logic
  • Positioning: European IT company delivering digital solutions for business
  • Formulated mission, vision and five core company values
  • Defined brand voice & tone (4 principles with examples)
  • Target audience analysis and market positioning (UK/EU)

Website Development — 5 Pages

Internationalisation — 7 Languages

EN DE FR RU AR HI ZH
  • Centralised i18n.ts file with 300+ translation keys
  • Language switcher in the header of all tool pages
  • All 4 tool pages fully translated into all 7 languages
  • RTL support for Arabic
  • Brandbook: fully translated in RU and EN; all other languages → EN

Deployment & Infrastructure
